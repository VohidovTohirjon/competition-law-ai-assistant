An officer asks a question. The system finds the right article of the law and writes the answer. Every article, number and date in it is checked against the statute text before it reaches the screen.

Savol to‘rt jilddan o‘tadiA question passes through four volumes

Model qonunni yoddan bilmaydi. Har safar unga topilgan modda matni beriladi, yozgani esa o‘sha matn bilan solishtiriladi. Oddiy tizimlar uchinchi jildda to‘xtaydi. Bu yerda to‘rtinchisi bor. The model does not know the law by heart. Each time it is handed the matching article, and what it writes is compared with that text. Ordinary systems stop at the third volume. This one has a fourth.

Tizimning o‘z qoidalariThe system’s own articles

Qonunni o‘qiydigan tizim o‘zi ham qoidaga bo‘ysunadi.A system that reads the law is bound by rules of its own.

1-modda. Manbasiz javob yo‘qArticle 1. No source, no answer

Mos modda topilmasa, tizim shuni aytadi. Taxmin qilib yozmaydi.If no article matches, the system says so instead of guessing.

2-modda. Uydirma ko‘rsatilmaydiArticle 2. Inventions are not shown

Model qonunda yo‘q modda, band, son yoki sana yozsa, uning matni tashlanadi va qonunning asl parchasi chiqadi.If the model writes an article, clause, number or date the law does not contain, its text is dropped and the statute’s own passage is shown.

3-modda. Maxfiy hujjat tashqariga chiqmaydiArticle 3. Confidential files stay in

Qidiruv modeli o‘z serverda ishlaydi. Maxfiy hujjat tashqi AI xizmatiga yuborilmaydi.The search model runs on our own server. A confidential file is never sent to an outside AI service.

4-modda. Har bir amal yoziladiArticle 4. Every action is logged

Administrator, rahbar va xodimning ruxsatlari alohida, har bir amal audit jurnaliga tushadi.Administrator, manager and officer have separate permissions, and every action lands in the audit log.
