RRaqobat AI Assistant

Raqobat qo‘mitasi uchunFor a competition authority

Qonunni topadi. Dalilini ko‘rsatadi. Finds the law. Shows the proof.

Xodim savol beradi, tizim kerakli qonun moddasini topadi va javob yozadi. Javobdagi har bir modda, son va sana qonun matni bilan solishtiriladi, shundan keyingina ekranga chiqadi. An officer asks a question. The system finds the right article of the law and writes an answer. Every article, number and date in that answer is checked against the statute text before it reaches the screen.

Ichida nima borWhat it does

To‘rtta ish jarayoni, hammasi bitta oynada. Rasmlar ishlab turgan ilovadan olingan, bosib kattalashtirish mumkin. Four workflows in one window. The images are from the running application; click any of them to enlarge.

01

Huquqiy savolLegal question

Xodim «Ustun mavqeni aniqlash mezonlari qanday?» deb so‘radi. Tizim 13-moddani topdi va mezonlarni qonundagi tartibda, to‘liq va har bir band yonida manba belgisi bilan chiqardi. Bu savolga model umuman chaqirilmadi: ro‘yxat qonun matnidan so‘zma-so‘z olinadi. An officer asked “What are the criteria for a dominant position?”. The system found article 13 and listed the criteria in the statute’s own order, complete, with a source mark next to each one. No model call was needed for this question: the list is taken verbatim from the law.

02

Hujjat tahliliDocument analysis

Murojaat yuklanadi, tizim qisqacha mazmun, asosiy bandlar va bir-biriga zid joylarni chiqaradi. Hujjat bo‘yicha savol ham berish mumkin. Har bir fakt yonida u olingan parchaning raqami turadi, tasdiqlanmagan jumla ko‘rsatilmaydi. Upload a complaint and the system produces a summary, the key points and any contradictions. You can also ask questions about the file. Every fact carries the number of the passage it came from; an unverified sentence is not shown.

03

Javob xatiResponse letter

Murojaat va unga mos qonun moddalari asosida rasmiy javob xati loyihasi tuziladi: mavzu, murojaat mazmuni, huquqiy asos, ko‘rib chiqish natijasi, imzo. Word fayl sifatida yuklab olinadi, tashkilot rekvizitlari blankga o‘zi tushadi. From the complaint and the matching articles the system drafts an official reply: subject, what was asked, legal basis, outcome, signature. It exports to Word with the organisation’s letterhead filled in.

04

Rahbar paneliManager dashboard

Hujjatlar, topshiriqlar, kechikishlar va xodimlar yuklamasi bir ekranda. «Bugungi muammolarni ko‘rsat» degan savolga tizim bazadan hisoblab javob beradi, bu yerda ham model ishlatilmaydi. Documents, tasks, overdue items and staff workload on one screen. Ask “show today’s problems” and the answer is computed from the database, again without a model.

Savol to‘rt bosqichdan o‘tadiEvery question passes four stages

Model qonunni yoddan bilmaydi. Har safar unga topilgan modda matni beriladi va yozgani shu matn bilan solishtiriladi. The model does not know the law by heart. Each time it is handed the matching article, and what it writes is compared with that text.

01

Yo‘naltirishRouting

Savol qaysi yo‘lga ketishini hal qiladi. Ba’zi savollarga model umuman kerak emas.Decides where the question goes. Some questions need no model at all.

02

QidiruvRetrieval

Matnni ma’no bo‘yicha qidiradi: bge-m3 va pgvector. 115 parchadan 3 tasini tanlaydi.Searches by meaning with bge-m3 and pgvector. Picks 3 of 115 chunks.

03

YozishWriting

Til modeli faqat berilgan matndan javob tuzadi: gpt-oss-120b.A language model writes the answer from the supplied text only: gpt-oss-120b.

04

TekshirishVerification

Modda, band, son, sana va iqtibos qonun matnida bormi. Tasdiqlanmasa, model matni ko‘rsatilmaydi.Is every article, clause, number, date and quote in the statute? If not, the model text is dropped.

Odatdagi RAG uchinchi bosqichda tugaydi. To‘rtinchisi shu loyihaning asosiy farqi: model xato qilsa ham, foydalanuvchiga uydirma modda yetib bormaydi. A typical RAG pipeline ends at stage three. Stage four is what sets this project apart: even when the model slips, an invented article never reaches the user.

Nega unga ishonish mumkinWhy it can be trusted

Manbasiz javob yo‘qNo source, no answer Mos modda topilmasa, tizim shuni aytadi. Taxmin qilib yozmaydi. If no article matches, the system says so instead of guessing.
Uydirma ushlanadiInventions are caught Umumiy rejimda model qonun nomi yoki jarima summasini aytsa, javob butunlay almashtiriladi. In general mode, if the model names a law or a fine, the whole answer is replaced.
Ma’lumot tashqariga chiqmaydiData stays in-house Qidiruv modeli o‘z serverda ishlaydi. Maxfiy hujjat tashqi xizmatga yuborilmaydi. The embedding model runs on our own server. Confidential files are never sent out.
Rollar va jurnalRoles and audit trail Administrator, rahbar, xodim. Har bir amal audit jurnaliga yoziladi. Administrator, manager, officer. Every action lands in an audit log.
